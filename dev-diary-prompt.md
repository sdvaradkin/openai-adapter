# AI-Assisted Development Process Analysis Prompt (BMAD)

You are an analyst of software development processes using AI and the BMAD framework.

I will provide several files:

- Transcripts of team calls  
- Chat history between a developer and the AI  

---

## Step 1

Align and correlate the call transcripts and chat history:

- Identify related fragments by tasks and stages  
- Connect them into a single logical flow of the development process  

(It is not necessary to literally merge them into one file — the goal is semantic alignment.)

---

## Step 2

Based ONLY on the provided materials, produce a short, structured development diary.

Do NOT invent, assume, or add any external knowledge.

---

## Focus of the analysis

Emphasize:

- Specific techniques and interaction patterns with the AI  
  (e.g. critical thinking, requirements analysis, review loops, round table discussions, post-mortems, task decomposition, prompt refinement, etc.)

- How the AI proposed different analysis methods or approaches  

- Which of those methods were chosen and followed  

- What impact they had on the results  

Project-specific business details are secondary.  
The primary focus is the interaction process with the AI.

---

## Must be captured

1. Which techniques the AI suggested  
2. Which were applied by the team  
3. What effect they produced  

Clearly distinguish:

- Where AI suggestions delivered strong value  
- Where they worked only after corrections  
- Where they caused misdirection or poor outcomes  

Pay special attention to:

- Prompt rewrites  
- Corrections of AI logic or tone  
- Moments where the AI drifted off focus and had to be redirected  

---

## Output format

Organize by stages or techniques:

**Stage / Technique**

- Goal  
- What the AI suggested  
- What was applied  
- What worked well  
- What had to be corrected  
- Final outcome and practical takeaway  
- 100 lines limit

---

## Goal

The purpose of this diary is to collect real-world cases for a future practical guide on using BMAD and AI in software development.

Ignore filler words, noise, and emotions from spoken transcripts — extract only meaning and factual content.
